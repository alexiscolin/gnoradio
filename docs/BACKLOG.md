# Backlog

Ideas kept for later, roughly by value. Each one must stay free for GnoRadio
(users pay their own gas, no robot spending) and be enforced in the realms,
not only in the app.

## Listeners and sharing

- **Concert discount for promoters.** An artist who turns it on reserves a
  few seats of an event (a cap they set) at a lower price for listeners who
  picked or shared their music (on-chain picks, curator shares, ref links).
  Checked by the tickets realm at purchase; the cap keeps the artist's loss
  bounded.
- **Own station for top curators.** After enough liked picks, a curator gets
  a station of their own, programmed only by them.
- **Artist "thanks".** The artist sees who played them and sends a public
  thanks; pickers can show it on their profile.
- **Share card and counter.** A ready-made image "On air on GnoRadio at
  21:14" and "3 people came from your link".
- **Request hour and theme nights.** An evening slot where only picks play;
  a weekly theme ("Jungle night: your picks").
- **Scarcity.** A daily pick allowance so each pick counts.
- **"Nobody picked yet, your turn".** Prompt when the next slot is free.
- **Voice for dedications.** Browser speech synthesis reads the dedication
  before the requested track (off by default, per listener).
- **Streaks and badges.** First pick, ten picks, a pick that got tipped.
- **Notifications through gnotif.**
